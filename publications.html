publications

Both papers come from my undergraduate research on automated reasoning and strategy synthesis at Jinan University.

2020

  • IJCAI'20
    Automatic Synthesis of Generalized Winning Strategies of Impartial Combinatorial Games Using SMT Solvers
    Kaisheng Wu, Liangda Fang, Liping Xiong, Zhao-Rong Lai, Yong Qiao, Kaidong Chen, and Fei Rong
    In Proceedings of the 29th International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence (IJCAI), pp. 1703–1711, 2020
    PDF DOI
    We formalize impartial combinatorial games (e.g., Nim, Chomp) in a logical framework based on linear integer arithmetic, then use SMT solvers to synthesize a winning formula that exactly characterizes the winning states, and from it derive generalized winning strategies that hold for possibly infinitely many game instances. Experiments on several games show the approach is effective.
