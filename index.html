Yong Qiao (乔勇)

SDE I at Amazon Web Services · Causal Discovery · LLM Agents · Prompt Engineering

Hi! I am Yong Qiao, a Software Development Engineer (SDE I) at Amazon, working in AWS Connect Decision (AWS Supply Chain). My interests lie in causal discovery and the development of LLM-based agents, including prompt engineering — designing the instructions, tools and workflows that make agents reliable.

I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Jinan University (2016–2020), where I worked on automated reasoning and strategy synthesis for combinatorial games, leading to papers at IJCAI 2020 and AAMAS 2020. I then earned my M.S. in Computer Science at Syracuse University (2021–2022), and joined Amazon in September 2025.

Outside of work I play badminton, read philosophy, and write essays on Substack (English) and WeChat (Chinese) — see interests.
